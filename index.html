🤖 How Mellow works

1. Your webcam stream is read inside the browser.
2. A small neural network (face-api.js, TinyFaceDetector + expression model) finds your face and scores seven expressions.
3. Results are smoothed over about 8 frames and only accepted when confidence is at least 55%, so the emotion doesn’t flicker.
4. The recommendation engine mixes emotion, language, genre and your likes and skips.
5. It builds a YouTube search such as “hindi happy dance songs”.

Built as a college AI/ML project. The model reads expressions, not feelings, and can be wrong.

🔒 Privacy

Camera frames are processed in your browser. They are never stored, recorded or uploaded by this app. “Stop Camera” ends the stream and the analysis immediately.

Likes, skips, your name and the mood history live only in this session and disappear on reload.

YouTube and Brave open as ordinary search links in a new tab. There is no scraping and no tracking.
